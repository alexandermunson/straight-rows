## Wider scan and break points of the sensitive thresholds

The table above is generated by `scripts/stability.py`; this section is hand-maintained from a wider single-parameter scan
(factors of roughly 0.25x to 3x on 30 parameters). Only these settings change the dev score or the class/tractor list of
the evaluation detections:

| parameter | default | first value that breaks | what breaks | observed value it must stay clear of |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mp_dip_db` | 6.0 | 8.0 | dev TRN-11 multipath representative missed (-2.92) | its dip depth is about -7.7 dB (median over the dip); a typical pass is about -8 dB |
| `mp_dip_max_db` | 16 | 10 | dev TRN-12 representative missed (-2.92) | its dip depth is about -11 dB |
| `mp_evt_min_s` | 8 | 18 | every multipath report disappears (-7.5) | dips last 15-17 s |
| `link_gap_s` | 300 | 120 | dev TRN-10 link fault breaks into fragments (-4.25); the eval link fault shrinks to 5 min | the link flickers: about 90 s down every 4 min |
| `regional_drop_db` | 3.0 | 8.0 | eval 18:30 jam splits into a T3 jam plus a T1 equipment fault | the least-affected machine there (T2) loses 5.5 dB |
| `cn0_drop_db` | 4.0 | 8.0 | one extra eval equipment detection inside the 02:12 jam | - |

`s4_high` is no longer on this list. The evaluation storm's S4 mean over the event is 0.45 (default threshold 0.30); if the
threshold is raised past it, or the S4 feed is missing, the storm still classifies as SPACE_WEATHER from Kp 6.8, the storm
advisory in force and its uniform 3-hour profile. `min_cn0_dip_db` up to 18 no longer adds a dev false positive.
Changes that only move an onset by <= 40 s (`bin_s` 5-60 s, `xte_abs_m` 2-3 m, `link_bad_frac` 0.3-0.95) are not listed.

Reading: the tightest margin is `mp_dip_db` (about 1.7 dB on the dev representative's depth). Everything else tolerates
+/-25 % with no change at all, and most tolerate +/-50 %.
Caveat: the dev and eval signals are simulated with large effect sizes, so wide margins are expected; this report cannot show
robustness to real receiver noise.
